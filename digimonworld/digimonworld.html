Your digimon in digimon world will be some sort of tamagotchi, he needs to be taken care of. His needs will be shown as a bubble on top of his head, or you can see them in the digimon menu.

Your digimon can get hungry, sleepy, tired and he needs to poop. He also has a mood bar (he can be happy or get angry). You can praise or discipline your digimon.

Digimons have lifespawn, which means it will die after a certain amount of time. If they lose a battle, you will lose one heart. Digimon has three hearts, after you lose all hearts, your digimon will also perish and turn into an egg.

Even if the digimon has that many needs, he won't die if you don't feed him or not let him sleep. The only way to make your digimon live less is when he's not happy or he's angry.

The sole purpose for taking care of your digimon is to make him evolve into a certain digimon. Check evolutions>requirements for more information about this.

Things like not feeding or skip sleeping will anger your digimon, but you can increase your digimon happiness by praising, or giving him certain items like blue apple or happymushrm: Items list. You can also discipline your digimon.

The only purpose of the digimon needs is for evolving into certain digimon. Depending on the stats, discipline, hapinnes and other values your digimon will evolve into one digimon or another (check evolutions for more information)

MOOD

Every 3 or 4 hours or so there is a check and depending how un/happy you are, you lose life span depending of it. as long as your happiness is 90% or higher you will live out your full life.

By using the "scold" command in the menu you "punish" your Digimon for the action he just took. If this scolding was reasonable he gains happiness and discipline. In case he did not do anything that required you to scold him then his happiness will go down while his discipline goes up. Refusing to consume an item. Pooping on the floor. The act of moving, be it you or your Digimon, disables the opportunity of a reasonable scold.

LIFESPAWN

if your happiness is rock bottom since birth you will probably die around age 8-9 if your happiness is 90% or higher whole life you will live until 15.

Your digimon will evolve after a certain amount of time depending on the current form of your digimon. Every digimon has a life spawn of 360h (15 days) (Check basics > lifespawn for more info) This is how much they take until the next evolution: 

Baby   🡆  in training = 6h
In training   🡆  rookie = 24h
Rookie  🡆  champion = 72h
Champion  🡆  ultimate = 144h

Note 1: Any Rookie will be forced to evolve into Numemon after 96 hours or more.

Note 2: Any Champion will evolve into Vademon at exactly 360 hours.

Note 3: After evolution, care mistakes and battles will reset to 0

Your digimon will evolve into different digimon depending on certain requirements. These requirements are:

Stats

Care mistakes

Weight : Food and restaurant

Bonus

Note 1: You only need to fulfill 3 requirements to digievolve. For the bonus requirement (check 4 evolution tool) you only need to fulfill 1 of the conditions inside that box for it to work.

Note 2: After evolution the care mistakes and battles will be reset to 0.

Note 3: The moves dynamite kick and horizontal kick count as twice for the bonus requirement for evolution.

You increase your stats by training your digimon or giving them an item (check training for more information). Your digimon gains weight every time he eats food, and loses weight when he poops, gets tired or sleeps (food also gives energy). For bonus we have discipline, happiness, techniques learned, battles, and other special characteristics. Care mistakes means you didn't take care of your digimon needs.

CARE MISTAKES

Skip feeding

Skip sleeping

Not pooping in a toilet.

EXCEPTION: If you sleep while your digimon is hungry or needs to poop you won't get a care mistake (next day the poop will be there, the virus bar will still increase)

Note 1: If the starvation timer drops to 0 during a training session or while resting at Punimon, Centarumon or Kuwagamon, you will get an additional care mistake.

Here is this fantastic tool where you can introduce your digimon stats to see which digimon you will get and the requirements needed for evolving: Evolution tool

You introduce your current stats of your digimon on the yellow box, and it will highlight a digimon if you are eligible for evolving. You can hover over every requirement to see the specific value. These simbols mean: ≤ less or equal than (number), ≥ greater or equal than (number)

There are other means to evolve a digimon which are through items or special conditions. Check it out here: Special conditions // Items

Some digimon have preferences for evolution (for example, if you have a biyomon you have more chances to evolve to birdramon. You can check this on the evolution tool, bonus requirement.

Every digimon can learn moves. The moves that the digimon can learn are based on the digimon type (fire-fight..). Once you learn a move, you have it forever, and once you get another digimon, you will be able to use it if the digimon is the specific type. There are 57 techniques you can learn.

Your digimon can learn moves in two different ways: By seeing the move while fighting an enemy digimon, or through training brains. Not because you see the move means you will learn it, it's based on chance. The moves you learn from brains are also a matter or chance. You learn the moves by brains in order, you can check the order in the moves tool tab: brain learning.

When you train brain at the gym, you have a chance to learn a move at 150, 250, 350, 450, 550, 600, 650, 700, 750, 800, 850, 900, 950 and if you can get exactly 999.

You can check every attack that a digimon can learn, which digimon you can learn it from, the chances and more in this tool: Moveset tool

Note 1: The moves dynamite kick and horizontal kick count as twice for the bonus requirement for evolution.

Note 2: if your digimon is main type mech you have to learn attack POWER CRANE from fighting other digimon otherwise you won’t learn anything from training brain at the gym (it’s a bug)

I made this route with the purpose of beating the game casually and very easy. We can do two things: Evolve our agumon into bakemon by dying, bakemon can enter grey lord's mansion and learn many good attacks there and attacks in area that will benefit us in fights with more than one enemy. Or we can turn agumon into numemon to get monzaemon for free (ultimate digimon).

There's a place called toy town where if you bring a numemon, it will enter the plushie's zip and turn into monzaemon, so our goal is to turn our agumon into numemon.

I made the monzaemon route to get all the resources necessary to train our digimons into ultimate the fastest way possible. Bakemon's route is mostly the same as monzaemon but differs a bit here and there.

The route is optimised so you recruit as many digimon possible to get access to all resources fast, cheesing all the random digimons so you don't have to fight if you don't want. Hitting all the travel points in the way so we can travel right away once we recruit birdramon.

We can pick two options, either evolve agumon into a numemon so you can get a monzaemon (ultimate digimon) for free, or evolve agumon into bakemon by dying (Check route for more info). If you want to go for numemon, we are going to do more than 5 care mistakes and weight under 15kg so don't feed Agumon except when you train so he doesn't get tired, and don't use any chip on agumon. We also need to win 5 fights, all of these so Agumon doesn't digievolve into Tyrannomon. None of these matters if you want to evolve to bakemon, you can use chips on bakemon.

Praise your digimon until the bar is fully blue. Go back inside jijimon's house and talk to tokomon. He will give you these items. You can rest in the future if you talk to punimon. It doesn't heal you fully and it doesn't remove much tiredness. Leave the house and go west, you will arrive to the gym. Train speed 4 times and exit the gym to the bottom.

Greymon

Drops off. Chip

Once you have 15PP or more, Greymon will challenge to a fight once you exit jijimon's house. BE CAREFUL! He's really strong (5000hp) so be prepared with healing and a strong digimon. Always carry a portable potty with you to exit jijimon's house to avoid the encounter if you are not ready.

Seadramon

You can fish seadramon at 8am or 5pm with any rod.

Any kind of meat works.Once it hooks, wait around 3 seconds to press X. Once he's hooked time will stop and to start fishing you have to press X again. Tap the x button once the meter on the left goes blue. It takes about 2 minutes until seadramon gets tired so you can pull faster.

He can transport you to beetle land, teach techniques and give you an item. CAREFUL! Once he carries you to beetle land you won't be able to get the item or learn the techniques, so do that first (you could get the moves/item by other means as well).

Let's be friends! : Transport to beetle land and gives the flute to call seadramon.

Teach me a technique: You can fish, ask for a technique, and fish again until you have all of them. You can exit the screen and enter again right away to fish him again. You can learn:

Aqua magic

Winter blast

Giga freeze

I want an item: You will receive water bottle, an item to evolve your digimon into seadramon.

Kabuterimon

You need greymon recruited for recruiting kabuterimon. Talk to the one on the left and press the second option.

Kuwagamon

You can recruit kuwagamon at any time. Talk to the kuwagamon by the machines and pick the second option.

Tyranommon

Cross amida forest and you will encounter tyranommon. Fight him and he will go away.

He talks about a meteorite that you should get rid of. You can enter now ancient speedy region. Time goes faster in there so try to be quick. There's a secret path to the left when you arrive to the giant dinosayr skeleton. You will find a digimon, press ''crack it and check'' to fight him, defeat him and he will run away.

Go back where you came from and talk to tyranommon. He will join the city and will help at the restaurant. On the way back you can get moldy meat from that vending machine. It's called 'steak'. We will use it later to recruit frigimon.

Andromon

To recruit andromon you have to beat giromon so the sewers are back to normal so you can recruit numemon.

Talk to whamon so you can enter factory town. There will be two digimons stopping you from entering.

The digimons will be gone at 12am and 12pm. Once you make it inside the building they will be gone forever.

You can cheese the digimon if you walk close to the left platform. You can find HP chip and MP chip. Cheese the numemon by walking on the left edge. Enter the room and you will have to fight giromon.

After beating giromon, you can go to the room on top. Cheese the digimon by walking on the left edge both in and out. Items: Brain chip, auto pilot, def. chip. Exit and talk to andromon to stop the gas at the sewers.

Numemon

Go back to the sewers after andromon stops the gas machine and fight numemon. He will join the city afterwards. Drops HP chip. He unlocks the secret shop and sells enemy bell and enemy repel.

Now go back and talk to andromon and he will say that he needs some time to repair. Once you have visited the large shop, the arena, the hospital and the restaurant in file city go back and talk to andromon a couple of times and he will join the city. He stands outside birdra transport.

Giromon

After talking to andromon (you don't need to recruit andromon) go back to where giromon was and talk to him. He will join the city, unlocks the jukebox in the restaurant.

Fun fact: in NTSC versions the jukebox is bugged and causes your game to crash!

MetalMamemon

Random encounter (Ultimate, very strong!!) - Drops def chip

Enter factorial town from the pier in the back of the corner over and over and eventually metalmamemon will stand there. Defeat him and he will join the city. Becomes an alternative curling opponent.

Vademon

After seeing the marks at spore area once, read shellmon's bulletin board n.2. Go back and there will be more marks on the floor. At 45pp, read shellmon's bulleting board n.8 and go back to the location. Vademon will appear and joins the city. Helps at the restaurant.

Leomon

-Off. chip at ancient cave

You will see a cutscene of a patamon being chased and saved by leomon at gear savannah. Talk to leomon and he will tell you he's looking for something. At 45pp and meramon recruited, there will be an ancestors cave located in drill tunnel. Pick leomon's stone by interacting with it at the cave, and go back through drill tunnel to gear savannah, give it to leomon and he will join the city. Does nothing.

Vegiemon

On day 15, a rain plant will appear right by tanemon's side. Pick it and carry it all the way to tropical jungle. Interact with the little sprout and use the rain plant on it. Vegiemon will appear, say that you like veggies and he will join the city. He will give you three pieces of sirloin per day, and you can buy supercarrot and hawk radish from him.

Piximon

Drops mp chip

Cross the screen at tropical jungle from the top over and over until you see piximon. You have the option to fight him or not. Beat him in a fight and he will join the city.

You can randomly encounter him at the store. He sells the training manual for 50.000 bits. If you have the manual in the inventory you get better stats while training.

Ninjamon

Drops quick chip

You can find it on the way to kunemon's bed once you have someone in the secret shop. Accept the fight and beat him. Provides general information of items.

Etemon

Drops brain chip

very strong, 5000hp. At 50pp go south from kunemon's bed and etemon will appear in front of the door. He will do 30% of your digimon's max health as a surprise attack (it will never kill).

Win the fight and he joins the city. He sells golden bananas for 50.000 bits, an item that turns your digimon into etemon, west of the digimon bridge.

Metalmamemon

Drops def.chip

5000hp, enter and exit the second screen at factorial room over and over until you don't find the brown digimon. You have the option to fight, accept the fight and beat him and he will join the city. He's an alternative curling opponent.

Mamemon

Drops quick chip - Full potential can learn

5000hp. Go back and forth at panorama plains until there's no digimon. You will find mamemon, you have the option to fight. Beat him and he will join the city. Sells booster disks at the secret store.
